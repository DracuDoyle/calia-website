What is CALIA?

CALIA is a mobile application designed for non-verbal children with ASD (ages 6-12). Through AI-powered tools, it supports three fundamental areas of development: communication, learning, and self-regulation. Each interaction is analyzed and adapted intelligently to meet the unique needs of every child.

👦👧 Patient

The child who uses the app to communicate through pictograms, complete adaptive learning exercises, and self-regulate through interactive emotional tools.

🙎‍♂️🙎 Tutor

Parent or caregiver who monitors the child's progress, configures the app, and receives AI-generated reports with personalized recommendations.

Features

CALIA is structured around three core areas designed to support the daily needs of non-verbal children with ASD.

🗣

Communication

Children express themselves through AI-powered pictogram systems. The app learns from recurring phrases and usage patterns to suggest personalized communication shortcuts, making interactions faster and more intuitive over time.

AI

Analyzes usage patterns and suggests personalized pictogram combinations based on each child's communication history.

📚

Learning

Adaptive exercises in math, sequences, visual association, and comprehension. Each activity adjusts its difficulty in real time based on the child's performance.

AI

Monitors response time, errors, and accuracy to automatically increase or decrease difficulty, ensuring the child is always challenged at the right level.

🧘

Self-regulation

Children identify and express their emotions through pictograms, then engage in calming interactive games tailored to their emotional state.

AI

Analyzes touch interaction patterns and recovery time to generate reports for parents and therapists with personalized care recommendations.
