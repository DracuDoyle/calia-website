What is CALIA?

CALIA is a mobile application designed for non-verbal children with ASD (ages 6-12). Through AI-powered tools, it supports three fundamental areas of development: communication, learning, and self-regulation. Each interaction is analyzed and adapted intelligently to meet the unique needs of every child.

👦👧 Patient

The child who uses the app to communicate through pictograms, complete adaptive learning exercises, and self-regulate through interactive emotional tools.

🙎‍♂️🙎 Tutor

Parent or caregiver who monitors the child's progress, configures the app, and receives AI-generated reports with personalized recommendations.
